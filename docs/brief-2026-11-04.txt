Motioptic Website — Fix List for Developer
Nov 2, 2026 · @Hyder
Summary
The site looks good, but most of its words still come from the template it was built on. Real project names sit on top of another agency's case-study text, the testimonials are invented, and some numbers contradict each other. A client who reads past the homepage will notice.
The five fixes that matter most:
Replace all 7 case-study pages' text. Each one describes a different, fictional company (Agencify, Antony, ZCF, Candreva, Sotto, Tesla, Bruno) and quotes made-up results like "60% more inquiries". The Monkeflow page claims Tesla was the client.
Remove the fake testimonials. "Nathan Drake" is a video game character, "Oglivy BM", "Bruno" and "Aixor" are template brands, and the Thomas J. Watson quote is a famous IBM line, not a client review.
Hide the blog until there are real posts. All 7 posts are template text, one has a made-up author, and one is a copy of a case study.
Fix the numbers. The stats show different values to Google (30+, 100+, 10) than to visitors (54+, 132+, 99). Use one set of real numbers.
Rewrite the headline copy in plain words (Copy rewrites has ready-to-paste text) and fix the typos ("satisfication", "DETAILSS", "Yours satisfication is my reputation").
This brief covers all 24 pages on motioptic.com as of today: home, the services overview and 4 service pages, the works index and 7 case studies, the blog index and 7 posts, contact and 404.
