ANIMATION

We add motion where it helps: logo animations, explainer videos, scroll effects and micro-interactions.

What it is

Animation enhances storytelling and elevates engagement. We use it to make digital experiences feel fluid, emotional, and memorable — whether it's for websites, UI elements, or brand content. Every movement we create has meaning.

How we work

Our animation service covers microinteractions, page transitions, hover states, scroll effects, and custom motion graphics. Each animation is designed to enhance usability and guide attention without overwhelming the experience.

We focus on performance-friendly animations, crafted using CSS, GSAP and Lottie. Everything is optimized for speed and compatibility — ensuring your site stays smooth and responsive across browsers and devices.

What you get

Deliverables include motion guidelines, reusable animation presets, and export files (e.g., JSON, MP4, SVG) tailored for web and mobile use. You’ll also receive implementation notes or embed-ready snippets for easy handoff.

Depending on your needs, we can include storyboard mockups, animation timing charts, and visual QA rounds. Post-delivery support is available for testing, fine-tuning, or adding future animations as your product evolves.
