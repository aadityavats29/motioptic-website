Privacy

Updated 4 November 2026.

Project enquiries

If you use our form, we collect your name, email, chosen service and project description, along with any budget and start-date details you choose to share. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

We use these details to review and respond to your enquiry. Enquiries may be sent to our Microsoft 365 business inbox or stored by the receiving service used for this website so we can respond. Submitting this form does not sign you up to a mailing list.

Website operation

The site loads its fonts and media locally. When enabled on our production website, we use Vercel Web Analytics to understand page visits. We may record that an enquiry was submitted, but we do not include form contents in analytics. Our analytics integration removes query strings and fragments from page URLs. No advertising script is installed. Hosting providers may keep technical access logs to operate the service.

Your information

To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have sent us, contact hyder@motioptic.com. We will discuss your request and any information needed to identify your enquiry.

Other websites

Links to LinkedIn and other external sites are governed by those sites' own privacy practices.
