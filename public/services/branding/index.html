BRANDING

We build a brand you can use everywhere: logo, colours, type and a simple guide so it stays consistent.

What it is

Your brand is how people recognise and remember you. We start by learning your story, your customers and your competitors, then design an identity that fits.

How we work

We design a logo with primary, secondary and icon versions, pick your colours and fonts, and write a style guide with clear do's and don'ts. Revisions are included at every stage.

What you get

Logo files in PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF, editable source files, and a brand guide with your fonts, colour codes (HEX, RGB, CMYK) and spacing rules. Depending on the package, we add social media headers, a favicon and mockups.
